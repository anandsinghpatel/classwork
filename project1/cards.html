Deer

A deer is a beautiful and gentle wild animal. It has four long legs, two ears, and a short tail. Male deer usually have antlers on their heads. Deer mostly eat grass, leaves, fruits, and plants. They are fast runners and can jump high. Deer usually live in forests and grasslands. They are known for their gentle nature and graceful movement.



Wolf

A wolf is a strong and intelligent wild animal that lives and hunts in groups called packs.
