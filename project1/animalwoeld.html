About Animals

Animals are living organisms that are an important part of our environment. They can be found in forests, oceans, deserts, grasslands, and many other places.

Animals are classified into different groups based on their characteristics. Some common groups include mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, and fish.

Animals play an important role in maintaining the balance of nature. They provide food, help in pollination, spread seeds, and support the ecosystem. We should protect animals and their natural habitats.
